SOP-CORR-014: Standard Operating Procedure for Piping Wall Thickness Corrosion Assessment

Section 1: Purpose
This procedure defines the minimum acceptable wall thickness and inspection
frequency requirements for carbon steel process piping at MRPL refinery
units, based on API 570 piping inspection code principles.

Section 2: Minimum Allowable Wall Thickness
The minimum allowable remaining wall thickness (t-min) for carbon steel
process piping in general hydrocarbon service is 6.0 mm. Any pipe location
whose measured current thickness falls at or below 6.0 mm shall be
immediately flagged for replacement and removed from service pending
engineering review.

Section 3: Remaining Life and Inspection Frequency
Remaining life shall be calculated from the corrosion rate observed between
two thickness inspections. Locations with a calculated remaining life below
10 years shall be flagged for increased inspection frequency and scheduled
for follow-up ultrasonic thickness testing within 6 months. Locations with
remaining life at or above 10 years may continue on the standard 5-year
inspection interval.

Section 4: Corrosion Rate Classification
- Corrosion rate below 0.1 mm/year: Low corrosion, standard monitoring.
- Corrosion rate 0.1 to 0.25 mm/year: Moderate corrosion, monitor closely.
- Corrosion rate above 0.25 mm/year: High corrosion, prioritize for
  re-inspection and consider engineering assessment for repair or
  replacement.

Section 5: Approval
Any piping location classified as high corrosion or with remaining life
below 10 years requires sign-off from the unit inspection engineer before
the equipment can be approved to continue in service.
